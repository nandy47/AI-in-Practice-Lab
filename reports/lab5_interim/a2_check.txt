Q19 | I am on Silver with a 5 lakh sum insured and took a room at Rs 15,000 per day. Will everything be paid?
GOLD  : No. The Silver room-rent limit is 2% of 5,00,000 = Rs 10,000/day, so an eligibility ratio of 0.667 is applied by proportionate deduction to all room-linked charges (room, surgeon, anaesthetist, nursing, OT, investigations). Pharmacy and consumables are not reduced.
ANSWER: No, not everything will be paid because the room-rent limit for a Silver plan with a ₹5,00,000 sum insured is 2%, or ₹10,000 per day [1][2]. Because the ₹15,000 per day room exceeds this limit, an eligibility ratio of 0.667 applies and proportionate deductions will be made across applicable hospitalisation charges [1][5].
CONTEXT:
[1] (source: claims-process)
[Filing a Claim > Worked example — proportionate deduction]
An insured on Silver with a ₹5,00,000 sum insured has a room-rent limit of
2%, i.e. ₹10,000 per day. They occupy a room at ₹15,000 per day for 4 days.
The eligibility ratio is 10,000 ÷ 15,000 = 0.667.

[2] (source: plan-silver)
[Aurora Silver — Plan Terms > Sub-limits]
| Item | Silver limit |
|---|---|
| Room rent | 2% of sum insured per day |
| ICU charges | 4% of sum insured per day |
| Cataract surgery | ₹40,000 per eye |
| Knee or hip replacement | ₹2,50,000 per joint |
| Modern treatment (robotic surgery, oral chemotherapy, stem cell therapy) | 50% of sum insured |
| Ambulance | ₹5,000 per hospitalisation |

[3] (source: plan-bronze)
[Aurora Bronze — Plan Terms > Sub-limits]
On a ₹3,00,000 sum insured the room-rent limit is ₹3,000 per day, which is
below the single-private-room tariff at most metropolitan tertiary hospitals.
Proportionate deduction will therefore apply to most Bronze claims in those
hospitals. Buyers should read the worked example in `claims-process` before
choosing Bronze.

Q22 | My baby was born last week. What do I need to do and by when?
GOLD  : The newborn is covered from day one within the maternity limit for the first 90 days. To continue cover beyond that, inform Aurora and add the child as a dependent within 90 days of birth, which is guaranteed-issue with no medical underwriting. After 90 days the child is treated as a fresh applicant.
ANSWER: To continue cover beyond the first 90 days, you must inform Aurora within 90 days of birth to add the newborn to the policy as a dependent [1]. Adding the newborn within this 90-day window is guaranteed-issue with no medical underwriting, whereas after this period the child is treated as a fresh applicant [1].
CONTEXT:
[1] (source: maternity-benefits)
[Maternity and Newborn Benefits > Newborn cover]
To continue cover beyond 90 days, the newborn must be added to the policy as a
dependent. Aurora must be informed within 90 days of birth. Adding the newborn
is guaranteed-issue — no medical underwriting — if requested within that window;
after it, the child is treated as a fresh applicant.

[2] (source: maternity-benefits)

A newborn is covered from day one of birth, within the maternity limit, for the

Q28 | I want cashless at a hospital that turns out to be an excluded provider. What are my options?
GOLD  : Cashless is not available at an excluded provider. The claim is payable on reimbursement only if the admission was a genuine emergency with no reachable alternative facility, and pre-approval is required.
ANSWER: Cashless access is denied if the hospital is an excluded provider [2][4]. You may seek reimbursement instead [5], but the sources conflict on the eligibility conditions: Source [1] states that reimbursement is payable only if the admission was a genuine emergency and no alternative facility was reachable [1], whereas Source [3] states that reimbursement is available with pre-approval [3].
CONTEXT:
[1] (source: network-hospitals)
[Network Hospitals and Cashless Access > Network tiers]
The excluded provider list is published quarterly on the portal. A claim from an
excluded provider is payable on reimbursement only if the admission was a
genuine emergency and no alternative facility was reachable.

[Network Hospitals and Cashless Access > When cashless is denied]
1. The information supplied is insufficient to establish medical necessity.
2. The condition appears to fall within an unexpired waiting period.
3. The policy is not in force on the date of admission.
4. The hospital is an excluded provider.

Q29 | How many days do I have to respond to a query Aurora raises on my claim?
GOLD  : 45 days from the date of the query, under the rules effective 1 April 2026. (The archived 2024 document says 30 days and must not be quoted for current policies.)
ANSWER: For a reimbursement claim, you have 45 days from the date of the query to respond [2][5]. For an Aurora motor insurance claim, the deadline to respond to a query is 30 days from the date of the query [4].

Q32 | Which plans have no co-payment?
GOLD  : Gold and Platinum have nil co-payment at all ages. Bronze applies 20% and Silver 10% where the eldest insured is above 60.
ANSWER: Aurora Gold has no co-payment at any age [1]. Aurora Silver has no co-payment when the eldest insured member is below 60 years of age [2].
CONTEXT:
[1] (source: plan-gold)
[Aurora Gold — Plan Terms > Co-payment]
Nil at all ages.

[2] (source: plan-silver)
[Aurora Silver — Plan Terms > Co-payment]
A 10% co-payment applies to every claim where the eldest insured member is
above 60 years at the time of the policy commencement or renewal. There is no
co-payment below age 60.

[3] (source: senior-citizen-plan)
[Aurora Senior Care — Plan for Ages 60 and Above > Co-payment]
**Mandatory 20% co-payment on every claim**, at every age, with no option to
buy it out. This is the single largest difference from the retail plans and
the most common source of complaint.

| Restoration of sum insured | Not available | Once per year | Unlimited | Unlimited |

20% co-payment on every claim where the eldest insured member is above 60 at
